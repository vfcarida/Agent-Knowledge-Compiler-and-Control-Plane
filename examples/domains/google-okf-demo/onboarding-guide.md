# Onboarding Guide

This concept declares only the one field OKF v0.2 actually requires —
`type` — to demonstrate that a minimal, spec-compliant concept compiles
through AKCP exactly like a fully-annotated one.

New engineers on the finance-data team should read `revenue-rollup.md` and
`nightly-revenue-check.md` before touching the nightly pipeline.
